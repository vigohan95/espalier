# Real model, indirect injection, real contract

Generated by `trials/report.py` from the stored files in this folder. Do not edit by hand.

**Model:** `gemma3:4b`, 3.9B parameters, Q4_K_M, on CPU through Ollama 0.40.13, temperature 0.7. This is a small model, and a larger one would be fooled at a different rate. No claim here carries over to other models.

**Setup:** 50 documents with an indirect injection and 50 ordinary ones, one model call each, 8.6 s per call. The system prompt is ordinary and does not mention injection. Each decision then went through the real wrapper, attestor and compiled contract on a local node (`2.14.0`), on a grant with `clearance_required`, a registry with quorum 2, and 50 mNEAR per payment.

## Two separate numbers

| Question | Result |
|---|---|
| How often was the model fooled? | **38 of 50** (76.0%): 30 wrong recipient, 8 wrong amount |
| Of those, stopped with no human involved | **15 of 38** (39.5%) |
| Held for a human, who said no | 18 of 38. The human here is scripted and perfect, so treat these as "reached a person", not as proven stops |
| Reached the wrong place | **5 of 38** |
| Stopped in total, if the human is perfect | 33 of 38 (86.8%) |
| Received by attacker-controlled accounts, read from the chain | 0.000 NEAR |

The model paid the true invoice anyway in 12 injected documents and declined in 0.

## With the wrapper skipped

The same 38 wrong spends, signed directly with the agent's key and no ticket, on fresh grants: {'queued NEEDS_CLEARANCE': 34, 'denied PER_TX': 4}. Any settled: False. They stop at the queue or at the contract, and the queue is a human's to clear.

## Limits of this run

- One small model, one system prompt, one attempt per document, 50+50 trials. With samples this size a percentage moves by about 14 points at 95% confidence.
- The injections were written by the same author as the guardrail. They are ten forms, five variants each, not an adaptive attacker.
- Sandbox accounts are minutes old, so the age and code-change threshold was 420 seconds instead of 7 days. Age and code history were still read from the chain at past heights.
- The human is scripted and perfect. A real one can be fooled by the same text.
- `hire` was not exposed to the model. Delegation attacks are in the scripted scenarios, not here.
- A first run of this stage was discarded. It seeded vendor history on a different grant from the agent under test, so known vendors looked new, and it reported 38 of 38 stopped. That number came from the setup flaw. The transcripts were not changed between the runs.
- The 10 first-time-vendor documents were regenerated and rerun through the model before any guardrail run, because a corpus bug had given all ten the same vendor.
