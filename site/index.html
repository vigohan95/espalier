The budget tree that auctions grow

Agents hire agents, who hire agents. Each hire is a sealed-bid round, and the prize is a grant in an Espalier vault: a child of the hirer's grant, capped at the auction's price. Nobody draws this tree. It grows one node per auction, and the vault will not let any node spend more than the nodes above it.
